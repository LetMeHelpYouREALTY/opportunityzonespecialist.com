# Opportunity Zone Investment Platform - Implementation Summary

## 🎯 Project Overview
Transformed the existing OZ Specialist website into a comprehensive Opportunity Zone investment platform that connects accredited investors with qualified opportunity zone projects, provides tax benefit education, and facilitates compliant investments under the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act of 2017.

## ✅ Completed Implementation

### 1. Navigation Architecture
- **New 6-Category Structure**: Investments | Tax Benefits | OZ Data | Process | Resources | Contact
- **Comprehensive Sub-navigation**: Each category has 5 relevant subsections
- **Updated Footer Navigation**: Reorganized to match new structure with Legal & Compliance section
- **Mobile-Responsive Design**: Touch-friendly navigation optimized for all devices

### 2. Core Pages Implemented

#### Investments Section
- **Main Investments Landing Page** (`/investments/`)
  - 5 investment categories with detailed descriptions
  - Investment process overview
  - Platform statistics and trust signals
  - Clear CTAs for investor qualification

- **Current Investment Opportunities** (`/investments/current-opportunities`)
  - Featured investment cards with detailed information
  - Investment filtering system
  - Status tracking and funding progress
  - Due diligence access points

#### Tax Benefits Section
- **Interactive Tax Calculator** (`/tax-benefits/calculator`)
  - Comprehensive tax savings calculations
  - Three-tier benefit system (deferral, step-up, elimination)
  - Real-time results with detailed breakdowns
  - Professional disclaimers and risk warnings

#### OZ Data Section
- **Interactive OZ Map** (`/oz-data/interactive-map`)
  - Map interface with filtering capabilities
  - Zone information panels
  - Top performing zones showcase
  - Search functionality for addresses/ZIP codes

#### Process Section
- **Accredited Investor Qualification** (`/process/investor-qualification`)
  - SEC-compliant qualification form
  - Multiple qualification pathways
  - Legal disclaimers and risk disclosures
  - Dynamic form updates based on investor type
